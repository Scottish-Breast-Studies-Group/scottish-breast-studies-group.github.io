We host regular webinars covering topics in breast disease and breast cancer research, diagnosis and management.

## Next Session  
**Update on the CESAR trial: comparative accuracy of contrast-enhanced mammography and MRI in assessing response to neoadjuvant chemotherapy**

* **Speaker:** Dr Sarah Savaridas, Senior Clinical Lecturer, University of Dundee; Honorary Consultant Breast Radiologist, NHS Tayside; Clinical Director, East of Scotland Breast Screening Service
* **Chair:** Professor Gerald Lip, Clinical Director, North East of Scotland Breast Screening Programme; Consultant Radiologist, NHS Grampian
* **Date:** Monday 5 October 2026
* **Time:** 13:10 – 13:40
* **Platform:** Microsoft Teams
* **Registration:** [Register here](https://events.teams.microsoft.com/event/b65cca12-2d29-4ebc-a3f2-cc04337b6e38@2e9f06b0-1669-4589-8789-10a06934dc61?source=copyLinkLegacyShareLinkDialog)  

*CESAR is a multicentre trial comparing contrast-enhanced mammography with MRI for assessing breast cancer response to neoadjuvant chemotherapy. Sarah will update us on the trial's progress, followed by questions. Please register in advance to receive the Teams joining link.*

## Programme

| Date | Speaker | Topic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5 October 2026 | Sarah Savaridas (Dundee) | **[CESAR trial](https://www.delegate-reg.co.uk/bsbr2024/sarah-savaridas):** A multicentre study comparing contrast-enhanced mammography with MRI for assessing breast cancer response to neoadjuvant chemotherapy. |
| Date to be confirmed | Rob Stein (to be confirmed) | **[OPTIMA trial](https://www.optimabreaststudy.com/):** Using tumour gene-expression testing to determine whether people with hormone-sensitive, HER2-negative early breast cancer can safely avoid chemotherapy after surgery. |
| 6 November 2026 | Iain MacPherson | **[HER2-RADiCAL trial](https://www.icr.ac.uk/research-and-discoveries/centres-and-strategic-collaborations/clinical-trials-and-statistics-unit-icr-ctsu/detail/her2-radical):** Investigating whether people with early HER2-positive breast cancer who respond completely to treatment before surgery can safely receive less drug treatment afterwards. |
| 7 December 2026 | Joel Allotey and Sylvia Kamya | **SPRINT Study (Cardio-oncology):** How anthracycline chemotherapy affects heart and skeletal-muscle function in people with early breast cancer, and approaches to reducing treatment-related impairment and fatigue. |
| January 2027 (provisionally 18 or 25 January) | Vassilis Pitsinis and colleagues (Dundee) | **[ICG tracer research](https://www.hra.nhs.uk/planning-and-improving-research/application-summaries/research-summaries/influence-indocyanine-green-node-fluorescence-study/):** Using indocyanine green fluorescence as a non-radioactive tracer to identify sentinel lymph nodes during surgery for early breast cancer. |
